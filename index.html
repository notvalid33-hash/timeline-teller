📥 How to export Timeline.json

By Dinesh

TT uses the Google Maps Timeline export from your Android phone. The file is processed locally in this browser and is not uploaded to GitHub or sent to TT.

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Tap Location → Location services → Timeline.
  3. Tap Export Timeline data.
  4. Tap Continue, choose where to save the file, then tap Save.
  5. Return to Timeline-Teller and tap Choose Timeline.json.

Google's menu names can vary slightly by Android phone/version.

📍 Time spent at places

All distinct Place IDs in the selected range are kept. The Google Timeline JSON normally contains a Place ID and coordinates, not a readable business/home name, so TT does not invent names. Use Open in Google Maps to inspect the corresponding place.
